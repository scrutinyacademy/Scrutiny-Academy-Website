Refund and Payment Support

Paid courses use monthly subscriptions, with 10% off a 3-month term and 20% off a 6-month term. Terms are paid upfront through Razorpay; renew at expiry to continue. No automatic deductions. The selected term unlocks only the purchased course. Existing purchases retain their original access.

Duplicate or completed payment without access

Do not pay again. Email scrutinyacademy@gmail.com with your registered email and Razorpay payment ID. We will verify the transaction and either activate access or resolve an eligible duplicate charge.

Refund requests

Send the request promptly with the registered email, payment ID, payment date and reason. Requests are reviewed according to the access already delivered, the circumstances of the transaction and applicable consumer law.

Security

Never send your UPI PIN, OTP, full card number or banking password to Scrutiny Academy. We will not request them.
