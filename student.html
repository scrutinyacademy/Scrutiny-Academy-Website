A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER

Built for students who want to understand, practise and improve.

Scrutiny Academy started with a simple idea: quality preparation should be clear, structured and genuinely useful to students.

Preparing for an exam can feel overwhelming—not because students lack potential, but because there is simply too much to study and too much noise around what actually matters. That is why we focus on turning the syllabus into focused learning resources, NCERT-based practice, revision tools and tests that help you identify exactly where you need to improve.

Every test you attempt, every mistake you analyse and every concept you revise is progress. Don’t study just to finish the syllabus. Study until you can trust yourself in the examination hall.

We will continue improving Scrutiny Academy based on what students actually need. When you leave a review or suggestion here, I genuinely want it to help shape what we build next.

Keep learning. Keep questioning. Keep improving.

Pramod SharmaFounder, Scrutiny Academy
