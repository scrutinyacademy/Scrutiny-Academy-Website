LAST UPDATED 13 SEPTEMBER 2026

Privacy Policy

Scrutiny Academy uses account information to provide student access, verify payments, save learning progress and provide support.

Information we process

We may process your name, email address, phone number, authentication identifiers, access status, payment reference identifiers, test history, bookmarks, mistakes and learning statistics. Razorpay processes payment-method information; Scrutiny Academy does not store your card, UPI PIN or banking credentials.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate your account, activate purchased access, sync learning progress, improve educational tools, prevent misuse and answer support requests.

Service providers and retention

Firebase is used for authentication and database services, Razorpay for payment processing and GitHub Pages for delivery of public website files. Information is retained only while needed to operate the account, meet legal obligations and resolve payment or security issues.

Your choices

You may export your learning progress from the platform. To request correction or deletion of account information, email scrutinyacademy@gmail.com. Some payment records may need to be retained where required by law.
