150 MCQs, 150 minutes, three locked sections of 50 questions/50 minutes; +4 correct, −1 incorrect, 0 unattempted.

IMPORTANT LIMIT

Not one paper for every surgeon

General Surgery candidates may need another group paper for a destination covered outside Surgical Group—for example Head & Neck Surgery is covered through the ENT Group.
